<Callout variant="tip" title="Track Overview & Exam Code: VCF 9.0 Architect">
The **VMware Cloud Foundation 9.0 Architect** exam tests your capacity to translate complex business objectives into resilient, secure, and manageable private cloud architectures. Mastery of the RCAR methodology (Requirements, Constraints, Assumptions, Risks) and the AMPRS-M framework is required.
</Callout>

Below is the structured roadmap for this certification. Each module contains key architecture mental models, deep-dive comparison tables, collapsible explanations for obscure concepts, and key takeaways.
