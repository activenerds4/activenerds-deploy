<Callout variant="tip" title="Track Overview & Exam Code: 3V0-24.25">
The **VMware Certified Advanced Professional - Cloud Foundation Kubernetes Services (3V0-24.25)** exam tests your ability to configure, manage, and troubleshoot enterprise Kubernetes platforms running natively on vSphere. Key areas include Supervisor cluster enablement, vSphere Namespaces, Antrea NSX routed networking, and declarative Cluster API deployments.
</Callout>
