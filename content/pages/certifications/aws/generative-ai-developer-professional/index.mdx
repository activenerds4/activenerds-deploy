## Exam Format at a Glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Scored questions | 65 |
| Unscored questions | 10 (not identified) |
| Question types | Multiple choice and multiple response (all correct options required) |
| Scoring | Scaled 100 - 1,000; passing score 750; compensatory (no per-section minimum) |
| Guessing | No penalty; unanswered questions are scored incorrect |
| Out of scope for candidates | Model development and training, advanced ML techniques, data and feature engineering |

<Callout variant="tip" title="Recommended Study Path">
Work Domain 1 first (31%), because retrieval, prompts, and FM selection reappear in every later domain. Then complete Domain 2 (26%) and Domain 3 (20%). Use Modules 15 - 18 to practice diagnosing symptoms across layers. After each module, answer its self-assessment checklist without opening the explanations.
</Callout>
