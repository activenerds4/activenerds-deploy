## Architectural Core Focus Areas

<AccordionGroup type="single">
  <AccordionItem title="Resilience & High Availability: Multi-AZ vs Multi-Region">
    <div className="space-y-3 py-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      <p>
        Across both Associate and Professional exams, AWS tests your ability to design fault-tolerant systems matching strict RTO and RPO requirements:
      </p>
      <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Multi-AZ Deployments:</strong> Synchronous replication for Aurora/RDS, Auto Scaling across 3 AZs behind an Application Load Balancer.</li>
        <li><strong>Multi-Region Active-Active:</strong> DynamoDB Global Tables, Aurora Global Database, and Route 53 latency-based routing with health checks.</li>
        <li><strong>Disaster Recovery Strategies:</strong> Backup & Restore (hours) → Pilot Light (tens of minutes) → Warm Standby (minutes) → Multi-Site Active/Active (zero/seconds).</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Enterprise Identity & Zero-Trust Access: IAM, STS & AWS Organizations">
    <div className="space-y-3 py-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      <p>
        Security is paramount across all AWS credentials:
      </p>
      <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Service Control Policies (SCPs):</strong> Guardrail maximum permissions without granting permissions directly.</li>
        <li><strong>Cross-Account Role Delegation:</strong> Use STS <code>AssumeRole</code> with external IDs to prevent confused deputy vulnerabilities.</li>
        <li><strong>Permission Boundaries:</strong> Delegate administration to developers without risking privilege escalation to administrative roles.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Generative AI & Modern Workloads: Bedrock, SageMaker & Vector Embeddings">
    <div className="space-y-3 py-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      <p>
        The latest blueprint revisions heavily emphasize serverless AI and retrieval-augmented generation:
      </p>
      <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Amazon Bedrock:</strong> Serverless Foundation Models (Claude, Titan, Llama) with automated Guardrails and Knowledge Bases.</li>
        <li><strong>Vector Stores:</strong> Amazon OpenSearch Serverless vector engine, pgvector on Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL, and MemoryDB for high-throughput similarity search.</li>
        <li><strong>SageMaker Endpoints:</strong> Real-time, serverless, asynchronous, and batch transform inference hosting with multi-model endpoints.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </AccordionItem>
</AccordionGroup>
