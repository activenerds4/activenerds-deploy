<Callout variant="tip" title="Track Overview & Exam Code: 2V0-17.25">
The **Cloud Foundation Administrator (2V0-17.25)** exam validates your technical competency in deploying, configuring, operating, and managing a VMware private cloud stack. Focus areas include Cloud Builder bring-up, workload domain provisioning, NSX Tier-0/1 routing, vSAN SPBM policies, and SDDC Manager LCM upgrades.
</Callout>
