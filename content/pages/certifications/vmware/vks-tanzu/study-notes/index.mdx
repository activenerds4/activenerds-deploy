<Callout variant="tip" title="Track Overview & Exam Code: 3V0-24.25">
The **VCF vSphere Kubernetes Service (3V0-24.25)** exam tests your ability to configure, manage, and troubleshoot enterprise Kubernetes platforms running natively on vSphere. Key areas include Supervisor cluster enablement, vSphere Namespaces, Antrea NSX routed networking, and declarative Cluster API deployments.
</Callout>
