## Architectural Core Focus Areas

<AccordionGroup type="single">
  <AccordionItem title="VCF 9 Fleet Management & Consolidated Architecture">
    <div className="space-y-3 py-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      <p>
        In VCF 9, VMware introduces centralized <strong>Fleet Management</strong>. Rather than treating each SDDC Manager instance as a standalone silo, multi-region and multi-instance environments are federated under unified operations and automation controllers.
      </p>
      <p>
        Key operational boundaries:
      </p>
      <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Management Domain:</strong> Houses all vCenter Servers, NSX Managers, and SDDC automation. Minimum 4 hosts with vSAN storage.</li>
        <li><strong>Workload Domains:</strong> Isolated policy domains hosting application clusters, each commanded by a dedicated vCenter residing in the Management Domain.</li>
        <li><strong>VCF Installer:</strong> Replaces legacy Cloud Builder to deliver streamlined imaging and validation workflows.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Networking Evolution: NSX VPCs vs Traditional Overlay Segments">
    <div className="space-y-3 py-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      <p>
        VCF 9 deeply integrates <strong>NSX Projects and Virtual Private Clouds (VPCs)</strong>:
      </p>
      <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li>Traditional NSX configurations required infrastructure teams to manually map Tier-1 gateways and segments for each application tier.</li>
        <li>NSX VPCs allow developers and Kubernetes namespaces to autonomously spin up self-contained networks with automated SNAT/DNAT, security policy isolation, and default route propagation.</li>
        <li>Eliminates central network bottlenecking while guaranteeing multi-tenant zero-trust isolation.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Storage Paradigm: vSAN Express Storage Architecture (ESA)">
    <div className="space-y-3 py-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      <p>
        vSAN ESA represents the most significant redesign of storage in vSphere history:
      </p>
      <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li>Eliminates traditional two-tier disk groups (cache SSD + capacity HDDs/SSDs).</li>
        <li>Uses a single tier of high-performance NVMe drives backed by a patent-pending Log-Structured Filesystem (LFS).</li>
        <li>RAID-5/6 erasure coding provides equivalent or superior performance to RAID-1 mirroring while reclaiming 30-50% storage capacity.</li>
        <li>Native compression and encryption occur at the top of the stack, dramatically reducing network traffic across the storage fabric.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </AccordionItem>
</AccordionGroup>
