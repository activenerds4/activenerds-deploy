## Finding Local Meetups

- **Meetup.com**: Search for "Kubernetes", "AI/ML", "DevSecOps" + your city
- **CNCF Community Groups**: [community.cncf.io](https://community.cncf.io) for K8s meetups
- **PyTorch Meetups**: Search on Meetup.com for PyTorch/torch gatherings
- **DevSecOps Meetups**: OWASP local chapters host regular meetups
