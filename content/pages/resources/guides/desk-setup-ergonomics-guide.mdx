## The Quick Reference

| Element | Ideal Position | How to Check |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Desk height | At elbow crease when seated/standing | Arms at 90-100° angle |
| Monitor top | At or slightly below eye level | No neck tilting |
| Monitor distance | Arm's length (20-30 inches) | Can read text without leaning |
| Keyboard | At or slightly below elbow level | Wrists straight when typing |
| Mouse | Same level as keyboard, within reach | No shoulder reaching |
| Chair height | Feet flat, knees at ~90° | Hips slightly above knees |
| Lumbar support | At curve of lower back | No slouching |

## Desk Height Formula

**Standing**: Measure from floor to your elbow crease. That's your desk height (typically 40-44 inches).

**Sitting**: Measure from seat pan to elbow crease. That's your desk height (typically 26-28 inches).

## Monitor Setup

- **Single monitor**: Centered directly in front of you
- **Dual monitors (equal use)**: Side by side, centered as a pair
- **Dual monitors (primary/secondary)**: Primary centered, secondary at 30° angle
- **Tilt**: 10-20° back from vertical
- **Height**: Top of screen at eye level — never higher

## Keyboard and Mouse

### Wrist Position
- Wrists should be **straight** (neutral) when typing
- Avoid wrist rests that push wrists UP — they should support, not elevate
- Negative tilt (front lower than back) is better than positive tilt

### Mouse Position
- Same height as keyboard
- Close enough that your elbow stays near your body
- Consider a trackball if you experience wrist fatigue

## Chair Adjustments

1. **Seat height**: Feet flat, thighs parallel to floor
2. **Seat depth**: 2-3 fingers between knee crease and seat edge
3. **Backrest**: Lumbar support at lower back curve
4. **Armrests**: At elbow height, shoulders relaxed
5. **Tilt**: Slight backward tilt (100-110°) for reading

## Quick Fixes (Free)

- **Monitor too low**: Stack books under it until at eye level
- **No footrest**: Use a box or stack of books
- **Keyboard too high**: Lower chair or use a keyboard tray
- **No lumbar support**: Roll a towel and place at lower back

<Note>
Start with the free fixes before buying anything. The most expensive ergonomic chair won't help if your monitor is at the wrong height. Fix the geometry first, then invest in equipment.
</Note>
