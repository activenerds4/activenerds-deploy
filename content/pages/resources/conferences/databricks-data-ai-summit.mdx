# Databricks Data + AI Summit Guide

**Data + AI Summit (DAIS) is the applied-ML conference for teams that ship pipelines, not papers.** Where GTC is hardware and NeurIPS is research, DAIS is the messy middle: lakehouse architecture, MLOps, feature stores, vector search, governance, and the operational reality of keeping models in production. If your day job is "make the data flow and the model serve," this is your event.

## 1. Who Should Attend

DAIS spans data engineering, ML, and analytics, but the core audience is the team that builds and operates the ML platform.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: data & ML platform teams in production">
If you build pipelines, manage feature stores, serve models, or govern data/ML at scale, DAIS is the most directly applicable conference on the calendar. The sessions are case-study heavy and the hallway track is full of people solving the same operational problems you are.
</Callout>

- **Data & ML platform engineers** — lakehouse, Delta, pipeline orchestration, MLOps, model registry.
- **ML engineers & applied scientists** — feature stores, vector search, RAG pipelines, fine-tuning at scale.
- **Data engineering leads** — pipeline reliability, governance, Unity Catalog, cost optimization.
- **Analytics & governance folks** — the data-governance and lineage tracks have grown substantially as regulation tightened.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
You're purely a researcher or you never touch Databricks — a meaningful share of content is Databricks-platform-specific. If you're vendor-neutral, weigh that against the genuinely good applied-ML and MLOps content.
</Callout>

## 3. Session Selection Strategy

DAIS runs parallel tracks across data, ML, and governance. Filter hard.

- **Prioritize production case studies** over roadmap sessions. "How we serve 10k RPS with vector search" beats "here's what's coming next quarter."
- **The MLOps and LLMOps tracks are the core.** Model registry, feature stores, evaluation, and RAG-in-production sessions are the highest signal.
- **Don't skip the governance/lineage track** if you operate in a regulated industry — it's where the real compliance-as-code content lives.
- **Reserve time for the expo hall's non-Databricks vendors** — the ecosystem (vector DBs, orchestration, observability) is well represented.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bring your pipeline architecture">
The hallway track at DAIS is unusually collaborative. Sketch your current pipeline (ingest → features → train → serve → monitor) on a napkin and you'll get 30 minutes of free architecture review from someone who's done it at scale.
</Callout>

## 5. Travel & Accommodation

San Francisco's Moscone Center is downtown and walkable, but SF logistics have quirks.

- **Hotel:** stay in SoMa near Moscone; the walk beats transit after a long day.
- **Flights:** SFO is the main airport; BART connects directly to downtown. Arrive Sunday for Monday training.
- **Ground transport:** walk or rideshare; SF parking is expensive and unnecessary.
- **Food:** SoMa has good lunch options but they're busy at noon — scout 2–3 within 3 blocks on Sunday.
- **Layers:** SF in June is famously cold and foggy ("June Gloom"); pack a jacket even if it's summer.

## 6. Physical Survival & Wellness

DAIS is a full week of dense applied content; the cognitive load is real.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Pipeline-brain is a real fatigue mode">
Data/ML sessions are dense with architecture diagrams and trade-offs. By Thursday your retention drops. Pace yourself like a long debugging session — structured breaks beat heroic cramming.
</Callout>

<ChecklistBox
  title="Conference-week wellness protocol"
  items={[
    "Wear supportive shoes — Moscone floors are concrete",
    "Alternate sit/stand; stand at the back for 2 sessions per day",
    "20-20-20 eye reset between diagram-heavy sessions",
    "Hydrate 1:1 with the coffee; SF summer air conditioning is aggressive",
    "Take a 10-minute outdoor walk at lunch — even in the fog, daylight helps",
    "Cap evening events at 2 nights; the training day is morning-fresh",
    "Pack a resistance band for thoracic mobility in your room",
    "Use the focus blocks: 50 min session, 10 min break, like a deep-work cycle",
  ]}
/>
