# Microsoft Ignite Guide

**Ignite is Microsoft's flagship — Azure infrastructure, AKS, Microsoft Fabric, and the Copilot/AI roadmap that increasingly defines the enterprise stack.** For teams on Azure or the Microsoft 365 + Azure combination, it's the annual architecture and roadmap event. It's heavily enterprise-skewed and AI-saturated, which suits IT and platform leaders more than pure researchers.

## 1. Who Should Attend

Ignite spans Azure, M365, Dynamics, and AI — broad, but the Azure and AI tracks are the technical core.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: Azure platform & AKS teams, plus enterprise AI adopters">
If you run AKS, Azure infrastructure, or are deploying Copilot/AI into an enterprise stack, Ignite's architecture and AI tracks are the roadmap. The AKS and Azure-infra sessions are taught by Microsoft engineers with real operational depth.
</Callout>

- **Azure platform & AKS engineers** — AKS, networking, storage, and the Azure-infra track.
- **Enterprise AI / Copilot adopters** — the Copilot and Azure OpenAI track is the enterprise AI roadmap.
- **Data engineers on Fabric** — Microsoft Fabric, Synapse, and the data-platform track.
- **IT & platform leaders** — Ignite is enterprise-heavy; the strategy and governance content suits leads.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
You're not on Azure/Microsoft stack — Ignite is deeply Microsoft-specific. If you're vendor-neutral or pure-research, GTC or DAIS serve you better.
</Callout>

## 3. Session Selection Strategy

Filter aggressively for your track; the AI volume is high.

- **Prioritize Microsoft-engineer sessions** in the AKS, infrastructure, and security tracks.
- **The hands-on labs are high value** — book early; they're capped and taught by Microsoft trainers.
- **Use the Copilot/AI track selectively** — pick 2–3 sessions relevant to your enterprise AI stack.
- **Reserve time at the AKS and Azure security booths** with concrete architecture questions.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bring your AKS architecture questions">
Microsoft engineers and Azure SAs staff the expo. A concrete question — AKS node autoscaling, Azure AD workload identity, multi-region failover — gets you a real answer and often a contact.
</Callout>

## 5. Travel & Accommodation

Ignite rotates between San Francisco and Chicago; both are walkable host cities.

- **Hotel:** stay near the venue (Moscone in SF, McCormick Place in Chicago); both are large and you'll walk a lot indoors.
- **Flights:** SFO/ORD are the main airports; arrive Sunday for Monday keynotes.
- **Ground transport:** walk or transit; parking is unnecessary and expensive.
- **Food:** both cities have good nearby options; scout them Sunday to avoid noon lines.
- **Layers:** SF in November is cool; Chicago in November is cold — pack accordingly.
