# Google Cloud Next Guide

**Google Cloud Next is the GCP equivalent of re:Invent — smaller, more AI-forward, and increasingly the place where Google's Gemini and Vertex AI roadmap lands.** For teams on GKE, BigQuery, or Vertex, it's the annual architecture and roadmap event. It's also the most AI-saturated of the hyperscaler conferences, which is a feature or a bug depending on your role.

## 1. Who Should Attend

Next skews toward GCP customers and AI/ML-heavy workloads.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: GKE platform teams & AI/ML engineers on Vertex">
If you run GKE, BigQuery, or Vertex AI in production, Next's architecture and AI tracks are the roadmap. The GKE and AI-infrastructure sessions are taught by Google engineers and include the operational depth that doesn't reach docs.
</Callout>

- **GKE platform & SRE engineers** — GKE, service mesh, networking, and the platform-engineering track.
- **AI/ML engineers on Vertex/Gemini** — inference, fine-tuning, RAG, and the AI infrastructure roadmap.
- **Data engineers** — BigQuery, data mesh, analytics, and the data-platform track.
- **DevSecOps & security teams** — the security track and Google's zero-trust content.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
You're not on GCP — content is Google-Cloud-specific. If you're multi-cloud, weigh the AI roadmap value against the platform lock-in of the sessions.
</Callout>

## 3. Session Selection Strategy

Single venue helps, but the AI volume means you must filter.

- **Prioritize Google-engineer sessions** in the GKE, infrastructure, and security tracks over customer-story keynotes.
- **The hands-on labs are high value** — book them early; they're capped and taught by Google trainers.
- **Use the AI track selectively.** Pick 2–3 Vertex/Gemini sessions relevant to your stack; skip the rest.
- **Reserve time at the GKE and security booths** — bring concrete architecture questions.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bring your GKE architecture questions">
Google SAs and GKE engineers staff the expo. A concrete question — node autoscaling under batch+service mixed workloads, multi-cluster service mesh — gets you a real answer and often a contact.
</Callout>

## 5. Travel & Accommodation

Mandalay Bay is a single venue, which simplifies logistics versus re:Invent.

- **Hotel:** stay at or adjacent to Mandalay Bay; the venue is at the south end of the Strip, far from other hotels.
- **Flights:** LAS; arrive Sunday for Monday keynotes.
- **Ground transport:** the venue is self-contained; you rarely need to leave. Use the monorail if you do.
- **Food:** venue food is overpriced; scout off-Strip options Sunday.
