# fwd:cloudsec Cloud Security Summit Guide

**fwd:cloudsec is the community-run, cloud-native-only security conference — no vendor keynotes, no marketing tracks, just practitioners sharing cloud and Kubernetes security.** If Black Hat is the broad security event and KubeCon's CloudNativeSecurityCon is the cloud-native slice, fwd:cloudsec is the pure, focused, community-priced version. For DevSecOps engineers, it's often the highest signal-to-noise event of the year.

## 1. Who Should Attend

fwd:cloudsec is narrowly scoped — cloud and Kubernetes security — which is exactly why it's high signal.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: DevSecOps & cloud-security practitioners">
If your day job is securing cloud workloads, Kubernetes clusters, or CI/CD pipelines, fwd:cloudsec is the most directly applicable security event on the calendar. Every session is on-topic, the speakers are practitioners, and the hallway track is full of people solving your exact problems.
</Callout>

- **DevSecOps & cloud-security engineers** — the core audience; supply chain, policy-as-code, runtime security.
- **Kubernetes security folks** — RBAC, network policies, pod security, admission control.
- **Detection & response engineers in the cloud** — runtime security, threat detection, incident response.
- **Security architects** — zero-trust, multi-cloud security posture, governance.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
You want broad security content (endpoint, network, physical) — fwd:cloudsec is cloud-only by design. Pair it with Black Hat for breadth.
</Callout>

## 2. Format & What You Get

Community-run means focused, affordable, and practitioner-led.

- **Single track or tight multi-track** — every session is on-topic; no marketing keynotes.
- **Practitioner talks** — speakers are people doing the work, not vendor sales engineers.
- **Community pricing** — a fraction of Black Hat or KubeCon; often includes meals.
- **Virtual option** — usually free or cheap, with recordings.
- **Workshops / hands-on** — often included, focused on cloud-security tooling.

---

## 3. Session Selection Strategy

Because the scope is tight, the selection problem is smaller — but the depth is higher.

- **Star sessions across the cloud-native security stack** — supply chain, runtime, policy-as-code, identity.
- **Prioritize case studies over tool demos.** "How we detected X in production" beats "here's our product."
- **Use the hallway track aggressively.** The audience is your peer set; conversations are the highest value.
- **Bring your real controls.** Speakers and attendees will discuss your actual setup if you ask.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The best hallway track in cloud security">
Because fwd:cloudsec is community-run and narrowly scoped, the hallway track is unusually high-signal — you're surrounded by people with your exact job. Bring your hardest cloud-security problem and you'll get a peer review.
</Callout>

## 5. Travel & Accommodation

Rotating venues in the US and EU; logistics are usually light because the events are regional.

- **Pick the nearest edition** — most attendees travel short-haul.
- **Stay near the venue** — community events aren't always in convention districts; check the map.
- **Coordinate with local meetup groups** — cloud-security Slack/Discord communities often organize shared travel.
