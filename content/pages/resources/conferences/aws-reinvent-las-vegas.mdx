# AWS re:Invent Guide

**re:Invent is the largest cloud conference in the world — 50,000+ people, multiple Strip venues, and a firehose of service launches and architecture content.** It's overwhelming by design. The value is real (deep architecture sessions, the hallway track with AWS engineers, and the roadmap you'll plan against for a year), but only if you plan ruthlessly. Unprepared, it's an expensive, exhausting blur.

## 1. Who Should Attend

re:Invent spans the entire AWS ecosystem, but it pays off most for teams running real production workloads on AWS.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: platform, SRE & DevSecOps teams on AWS">
If you architect, operate, or secure workloads on AWS at scale, re:Invent's architecture and security tracks are unmatched. The "chalk talks" and builder sessions — small, interactive, whiteboard-led — are where the real learning happens, not the 600-person keynotes.
</Callout>

- **Platform & SRE engineers** — EKS, networking, storage, and the operational tracks.
- **DevSecOps & security teams** — the security track and the AWS security announcements are the annual roadmap.
- **Data & ML engineers** — SageMaker, Bedrock, vector databases, and the AI/ML infrastructure launches.
- **Architects & engineering leads** — the architecture track and AWS solution architects (staffed everywhere) are the draw.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
You're not on AWS — a large share of content is AWS-service-specific. If you're multi-cloud or non-AWS, the ROI drops. Consider the free virtual pass or a regional re:Cap event instead.
</Callout>

## 3. Session Selection Strategy

With 2,000+ sessions across multiple venues, you must plan or you'll waste the week in transit.

- **Cluster sessions by venue.** Pick a hotel per half-day and stay there; cross-Strip transit will eat 30% of your time otherwise.
- **Prioritize chalk talks & builder sessions** over lecture sessions — interactive, capped, taught by AWS engineers.
- **Filter by track, then by "AWS engineer" speaker** for the deepest content.
- **Watch the keynotes for launches, then skip the rest.** The Monday re:Invent keynote is the roadmap; watch the partner keynotes at 1.5x later.
- **Reserve time at the AWS booths.** Service teams staff them; bring concrete architecture questions.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The re:Cap regional events are a hidden freebie">
If you can't make Vegas, AWS runs free regional re:Cap events worldwide after re:Invent — they replay the top sessions and you can ask local SAs questions. Excellent for teams.
</Callout>

## 5. Travel & Accommodation (Las Vegas)

The multi-venue layout is the single biggest logistical factor.

- **Hotel:** stay at the venue where most of your sessions cluster. The walk between Venetian and MGM is 45 minutes; the monorail helps but is slow.
- **Flights:** LAS is close to the Strip; arrive Saturday for Monday keynotes.
- **Ground transport:** the re:Invent shuttle buses between venues are free and faster than rideshare during peak. Use them.
- **Food:** venue food is overpriced; off-Strip is better. Scout options Sunday; lunch lines are brutal.

## 6. Physical Survival & Wellness

re:Invent is the most physically demanding conference on this list — Vegas, late November, 6+ venues, 50,000 people.

<Callout variant="warning" title="re:Invent will break you if you don't pace it">
You'll walk 6–10 miles a day between venues, sit in long sessions, and fight crowds. Combined with Vegas dehydration and late nights, expect a crash by Thursday without a strict protocol. Treat this like a multi-day on-call rotation, not a trip.
</Callout>

<ChecklistBox
  title="Conference-week wellness protocol"
  items={[
    "Wear the most supportive shoes you own — you'll walk miles on concrete daily",
    "Use the re:Invent shuttles, not rideshare — faster and you sit down",
    "Cluster sessions by venue to minimize cross-Strip transit",
    "Hydrate aggressively — Vegas air + AC + free drinks dehydrate fast; 3L+/day",
    "20-20-20 eye reset between sessions; casino lighting strains focus",
    "Get real daylight: step outside briefly morning and evening",
    "Cap evening events at 2 nights — re:Invent parties are legendary and relentless",
    "Pack a resistance band for thoracic mobility; 5 min in your room beats a stiff back",
    "Eat real meals on a schedule, not midnight buffets",
  ]}
/>
