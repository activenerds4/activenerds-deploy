# GitHub Universe Conference Guide

**GitHub Universe is the flagship developer conference celebrating developer experience, AI-assisted coding, and software supply chain security.** Held annually at the scenic Fort Mason Center on the San Francisco waterfront, Universe combines major technical keynotes with interactive demo domes, open-source maintainer lounges, and hands-on workshops.

---

## 1. Who Should Attend

Universe is designed specifically for:
- **Software Engineers & Team Leads**: Looking to master AI-assisted workflows, Copilot extensions, and automated pull request agents.
- **DevSecOps & Platform Engineers**: Implementing automated security scanning, code scanning, and supply chain attestation in CI/CD.
- **Open-Source Maintainers**: Networking directly with the GitHub engineering team and community peers.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: Engineers driving DevEx & AI adoption">
If your engineering organization is standardizing on Copilot, Actions, or Advanced Security, Universe offers direct access to the GitHub product managers and engineers architecting these tools.
</Callout>

---

## 2. Format & Tracks

The two-day technical schedule focuses on four core pillars:

1. **AI & Copilot Workflows**: Next-generation Copilot Workspace, autonomous agents, and AI pair programming.
2. **Developer Experience & Productivity**: Optimizing CI/CD with GitHub Actions, reducing context switching, and measuring developer velocity.
3. **Open Source & Community**: Sustainable maintainer funding, governance, and community health.
4. **Security & Supply Chain Protection**: Secret scanning, Dependabot automation, and software bill of materials (SBOM) generation.

---

## 3. Travel & Venue Logistics (San Francisco)

- **Venue**: Fort Mason Center for Arts & Culture (San Francisco, CA).
- **Airport**: San Francisco International Airport (SFO) or Oakland International Airport (OAK).
- **Public Transit**: Take BART to Embarcadero station, then take the historic Muni F-Line or Muni bus route 28/49 directly to Fort Mason.

---

## 4. Physical Movement & Stay-Active Tips

- **Waterfront Run to the Golden Gate Bridge**: Fort Mason connects directly to the San Francisco Bay Trail. Step out before morning keynotes for a flat, scenic 2-mile run through Crissy Field to the base of the Golden Gate Bridge.
- **Marina Fog Layers**: Late October in San Francisco brings morning marine fog (52–62°F) followed by sunny afternoons. Wear layers and pack a lightweight windbreaker for the waterfront campus.
- **Quiet Outdoor Break Zones**: The Great Meadow at Fort Mason provides green hills overlooking Alcatraz and the bay — ideal for stepping away from the exhibition noise between sessions.
