# CES Las Vegas Guide

**CES is the world's most influential technology event — 140,000+ attendees, 4,000+ exhibitors across 2.5 million square feet of Las Vegas exhibit halls.** While traditionally viewed as a consumer gadget show, CES has transformed into the premiere showcase for **embodied AI, autonomous systems, developer health wearables, edge silicon, and robotics**.

For engineering leaders, system architects, and tech practitioners, CES offers a first look at the hardware primitives that will power the next generation of ambient computing and developer workstations.

## 1. Who Should Attend

CES spans the entire global electronics supply chain, but for software developers and systems engineers, strategic focus is required.

<Callout variant="tip" title="High ROI: AI hardware engineers, IoT/edge developers & health-tech builders">
If your work touches on-device neural processing units (NPUs), smart sensor telemetry, wearable biometric integration, or robotics SDKs, CES provides direct face-to-face access to the silicon vendors and hardware design teams before products hit mass production.
</Callout>

- **AI & Embedded Systems Engineers** — Qualcomm, NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, and RISC-V silicon roadmaps and local NPU accelerators.
- **Health-Tech & Wearable Developers** — Oura, Whoop, continuous glucose monitors (CGMs), smart posture wearables, and desk wellness ergonomics.
- **Robotics & Autonomous Vehicle Engineers** — LiDAR, computer vision cameras, ROS 2 implementations, and automotive compute clusters.
- **Hardware & Gear Enthusiasts** — Next-gen OLED monitors, custom mechanical switches, ergonomic workstations, and ultra-high-refresh displays.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
Your focus is purely backend cloud infrastructure (e.g. Kubernetes CRDs, AWS IAM policies, or SQL query tuning). For pure cloud engineering, events like KubeCon, AWS re:Invent, or Google Cloud Next offer significantly higher density of relevant architecture sessions.
</Callout>

## 3. Key Engineering & Architecture Tracks

1. **AI Everywhere & Edge Inference**:
   - Focus on NPU acceleration, local LLM execution on mobile/laptop silicon, and privacy-preserving ambient voice interfaces.
2. **Digital Health & Active Workstation Tech**:
   - Smart desk integrations, motorized sit-stand sensor networks, continuous metabolic tracking, and blue-light circadian lighting systems.
3. **Eureka Park (Venetian Level 1)**:
   - The startup incubator zone featuring over 1,000 early-stage hardware startups. This is where unannounced developer gadgets and open-source hardware tools often debut.

## 4. Budget & Travel Hacks (Las Vegas in January)

- **Book Flights by September**: January flights into Harry Reid International (LAS) surge 3x as CES dates approach.
- **Use the Las Vegas Monorail**: A multi-day Monorail pass is the fastest way to travel between the MGM Grand, Caesars, Westgate, and the LVCC Central Hall, avoiding taxi queues completely.
- **Registration Deadline**: Register before early December. CTA pass prices increase steeply after the initial registration tier closes.
