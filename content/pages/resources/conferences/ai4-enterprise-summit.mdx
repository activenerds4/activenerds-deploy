# Ai4 Conference Guide

**Ai4 is the enterprise-AI adoption conference — less about how models work and more about how businesses deploy them responsibly.** If GTC is the engine and DAIS is the pipeline, Ai4 is the boardroom and the compliance office: AI strategy, governance, risk, vendor selection, and the operational reality of putting ML into regulated, real-world businesses. It's a good fit for the AI/ML engineer who's being pulled into "how do we adopt this safely" conversations.

## 1. Who Should Attend

Ai4 skews toward enterprise adopters — finance, healthcare, retail, manufacturing — rather than pure ML research.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: ML engineers pulled into AI strategy & governance">
If you're the technical person in the room when leadership asks "how do we deploy AI safely and what's it worth," Ai4 gives you the vocabulary, the vendor landscape, and the governance frameworks to answer well. Pair it with a deeper technical event for the engineering content.
</Callout>

- **ML/AI engineers in regulated industries** — finance, healthcare, insurance. The responsible-AI and governance content is the draw.
- **AI/ML leads & managers** — strategy, vendor selection, ROI frameworks, build-vs-buy.
- **Data & platform leaders** — the operational side of putting models into business processes.
- **Compliance, risk & legal adjacent to AI** — Ai4 is one of the few events where governance and engineering share a track.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
You want deep technical ML or infrastructure content — Ai4 is adoption and strategy focused. Go to GTC or DAIS for engineering depth; use Ai4 for the governance and enterprise-deployment lens.
</Callout>

## 2. Format & Tracks

Ai4 is a single-venue event with multiple tracks and a strong industry-vertical structure.

- **Industry tracks** — finance, healthcare, retail, supply chain, public sector. Sessions are case-study heavy.
- **Cross-cutting tracks** — responsible AI, governance, security, infrastructure, generative AI adoption.
- **Expo floor** — vendor-heavy; useful for build-vs-buy evaluation.
- **Workshops** — pre-conference half-day sessions on governance and applied AI.

---

## 3. Session Selection Strategy

Filter for your industry vertical and the governance track; skip the vendor demos.

- **Pick your industry track first.** The vertical sessions are where peers share what actually worked (and what didn't).
- **The responsible-AI / governance track is the differentiator.** Few conferences cover AI risk, evaluation, and compliance this seriously.
- **Use the expo for vendor evaluation, not learning.** Bring a shortlist of build-vs-buy questions.
- **Network across industries.** The cross-vertical hallway track is where you find transferable patterns.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bring your governance gaps">
If your org is wrestling with model evaluation, bias testing, or AI policy, the governance track and the hallway conversations with risk/compliance peers are worth the trip. You'll leave with a concrete checklist.
</Callout>

## 5. Travel & Accommodation (Las Vegas)

Las Vegas is logistically easy but physically draining — the Strip is designed to keep you indoors and gambling.

- **Hotel:** stay at or adjacent to the conference venue to avoid long walks in 40°C+ August heat. The Strip is longer than it looks.
- **Flights:** LAS is close to the Strip and cheap to reach; arrive Sunday for Monday workshops.
- **Ground transport:** walk indoors via casino connectors, or rideshare. Avoid midday outdoor walks in August.
- **Food:** venue food is overpriced; off-Strip restaurants are cheaper and better. Scout options Sunday.

## 6. Physical Survival & Wellness

Las Vegas in August is extreme heat plus windowless casino-convention spaces — a serious wellness challenge.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Vegas August is a wellness hazard">
Expect 40°C+ outdoors and aggressive air conditioning indoors. The combination dehydrates you fast and the windowless convention spaces compound cognitive fatigue. Hydration and daylight breaks are non-negotiable, not optional.
</Callout>

<ChecklistBox
  title="Conference-week wellness protocol"
  items={[
    "Hydrate aggressively — Vegas heat + AC + free cocktails dehydrate fast; aim for 3L water/day",
    "Wear supportive shoes — casino and convention floors are concrete; you'll walk miles indoors",
    "Never walk the Strip outdoors midday in August; use indoor connectors or rideshare",
    "20-20-20 eye reset between sessions; casino lighting strains focus",
    "Get real daylight: step outside briefly morning and evening (not midday) to anchor your clock",
    "Cap evening events at 2 nights — Vegas nightlife is relentless; the sessions are morning-fresh",
    "Pack a resistance band for thoracic mobility in your room",
    "Eat real meals, not casino buffets at midnight — your sleep and focus depend on it",
  ]}
/>
