# KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe Guide

**KubeCon EU is the spring counterpart to KubeCon NA — same gravity, different timezone, and a chance to meet the European half of the cloud-native community.** Held in April in rotating cities (London, Amsterdam, Paris), it's the venue where CNCF projects land their European case studies, where platform teams from fintech and telco share hard-won production lessons, and where the hallway track spans more languages than any other tech event.

## 1. Who Should Attend

KubeCon EU skews slightly more toward regulated-industry practitioners — European fintech, telco, and public-sector platform teams operate under constraints (GDPR, DORA, data sovereignty) that produce genuinely different architectures.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: European platform & regulated-industry teams">
If you run K8s under EU data-residency, financial compliance, or sovereign-cloud constraints, the EU edition surfaces case studies you won't hear at NA. The supply-chain, policy-as-code, and multi-tenant isolation tracks are especially strong.
</Callout>

- **Platform engineers & SREs in EMEA** — same core audience as NA, with a regional case-study lens.
- **Regulated-industry teams** — fintech, telco, healthcare, public sector. Sovereign cloud, data residency, and compliance-as-code sessions are more prominent here.
- **DevSecOps & supply-chain security** — CloudNativeSecurityCon EU co-locates here too; the European SBOM and cyber-resilience (CRA) content is ahead of NA.
- **OSS contributors** — the contributor summit and SIG meetings run the same as NA; many maintainers attend both, but EU is the easier one for EMEA contributors to reach.

<Callout variant="info" title="NA vs EU: which to pick?">
Content overlap is ~70%. Pick the one in your timezone for lower travel cost and jet lag. If you can only attend one and you work in a regulated European industry, pick EU. If you want the largest hallway track and the most US hyperscaler case studies, pick NA.
</Callout>

## 3. Track Strategy & Session Selection

EU runs 8–12 parallel tracks. The same selection logic applies — optimize for what doesn't record well.

- **Star 2 sessions per slot** in the app; you'll drop one when the hallway track pulls you in.
- **Prioritize EU-regional case studies** you won't see at NA: sovereign cloud, DORA compliance, data-residency architectures.
- **Reserve a slot per day for the hallway track.** The project pavilion and SIG booths are where you meet maintainers face-to-face.
- **Keynotes are skimmable** — watch at 1.5x later, use the slot for a walk and daylight.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The contributor summit (Tuesday)">
Free with your pass if you've contributed to a CNCF project. EU's contributor summit is smaller and more intimate than NA's — easier to actually talk to SIG leads.
</Callout>

## 4. Budget & Discount Hacks

EU pricing is in EUR and broadly comparable to NA once you account for currency. The travel math differs — intra-EU flights and trains are cheap, but cross-continent travel adds up.

## 5. Travel & Accommodation (EU)

EU convention cities are well-connected but have their own quirks.

- **Hotel:** stay within walking distance of the venue. European city centers are walkable; a 10-minute walk beats navigating unfamiliar transit after a 12-hour day.
- **Trains over planes where possible.** London, Amsterdam, and Paris are all on high-speed rail networks. The train drops you in the city center; the airport drops you an hour outside it.
- **Arrive Saturday** if crossing timezones — give yourself Sunday to acclimate before the co-located days.
- **Currency:** confirm your card has no foreign-transaction fees; venue food is overpriced in any currency.
- **Scout restaurants Sunday.** European convention districts empty after 6pm — know your dinner options before you're exhausted.

## 6. Physical Survival & Wellness

Same convention-center reality as NA, compounded by jet lag if you've crossed timezones.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Jet lag + concrete floors = a rough week">
If you've flown in from another continent, your circadian clock will be off for the first 2 days — exactly when the co-located days run. Force morning daylight exposure and cap evening screen time to shift faster.
</Callout>

<ChecklistBox
  title="Conference-week wellness protocol"
  items={[
    "Wear supportive shoes — convention-center floors are concrete, European cobblestones worse",
    "Stand at the back of 2 sessions per day; alternate sit/stand like a desk duty cycle",
    "20-20-20 eye reset between sessions: every 20 min, look 20 ft away for 20 seconds",
    "Get morning daylight — walk outside at breakfast to anchor your circadian clock after travel",
    "Hydrate 1:1 with the free coffee; convention-center air is dry",
    "Cap social events at 2 nights — the hallway track outvalues the 3rd party",
    "Pack a resistance band for 5 minutes of thoracic mobility in your room each evening",
    "If jet-lagged, take a 20-min power nap before evening events, not after",
  ]}
/>
