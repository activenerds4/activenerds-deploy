# All Things Open Guide

**All Things Open (ATO) is the largest open-source conference on the US East Coast — broad, community-priced, and increasingly the East-Coast home for cloud-native, AI/ML, and OSS practitioners.** It's the accessible alternative to the expensive Vegas/San Jose circuit: real content, real maintainers, a fraction of the cost, and a walkable downtown Raleigh venue. For many practitioners, it's the highest-ROI event of the year.

## 1. Who Should Attend

ATO is broad — open source, cloud-native, AI/ML, DevOps, security — which makes it a strong generalist event.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best fit: East-Coast OSS & cloud-native practitioners on a budget">
If you want KubeCon-grade content without KubeCon-grade cost, ATO delivers. The cloud-native, AI/ML, and OSS tracks feature real maintainers and practitioners, and Raleigh in October is pleasant and walkable. It's the best value conference on this list.
</Callout>

- **Cloud-native & Kubernetes practitioners** — the cloud-native track is strong and features CNCF project maintainers.
- **AI/ML engineers** — the AI track has grown fast; local and national speakers on applied ML and OSS AI tooling.
- **OSS maintainers & contributors** — ATO is community-oriented; maintainers are approachable.
- **DevOps & platform engineers** — the DevOps and platform tracks cover CI/CD, IaC, and observability.
- **Students & career-switchers** — community pricing and a friendly vibe make it a great first conference.

<Callout variant="info" title="Maybe skip if">
You need the deepest single-domain depth (e.g., pure K8s SIG-level content or pure research) — ATO is broad. Pair it with a domain-specific event for depth.
</Callout>

## 3. Session Selection Strategy

ATO runs multiple tracks across OSS, cloud-native, AI/ML, and DevOps. Filter for your stack.

- **Prioritize maintainer-led sessions** — CNCF project maintainers and OSS contributors give the deepest talks.
- **The cloud-native and AI tracks are the core** for this audience; the OSS track is the differentiator.
- **Use the hallway track.** ATO is community-oriented; maintainers and speakers are unusually approachable.
- **Bring your real architecture.** Practitioners will discuss your actual setup.

<Callout variant="tip" title="The best value hallway track on the East Coast">
Because ATO is community-priced and approachable, the hallway track is exceptional — you'll talk to maintainers and practitioners you'd never reach at a $2,000 event. Budget hallway time like you budget session time.
</Callout>

## 5. Travel & Accommodation (Raleigh)

Raleigh is one of the easiest host cities on this list — walkable, affordable, mild in October.

- **Hotel:** stay downtown near the Raleigh Convention Center; the area is walkable and safe.
- **Flights:** RDU is ~20 min from downtown; arrive Sunday for Monday keynotes.
- **Ground transport:** walk downtown or rideshare; parking is unnecessary.
- **Food:** downtown Raleigh has excellent, affordable options within walking distance.
- **Weather:** October in Raleigh is mild and pleasant — pack layers, not extreme-weather gear.
