# On-Call & Long Debugging Physical Reset Checklist

**The incident ends. Your back and eyes shouldn't be the casualty.**

A 4-hour debugging session or an on-call page at 3 AM is when ergonomics collapse — you hunch, you stare, you don't blink, you don't move. This checklist is the physical reset protocol for high-focus, high-stress sessions: protect your eyes, mobilize your spine and wrists, and handle the light when it's dark out.

## 1. Visual Reset (20-20-20)

<ChecklistBox
  title="Visual Reset (20-20-20)"
  items={[
    "Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds",
    "Set a recurring timer or use a focus tool to enforce the break (don't rely on remembering)",
    "Blink deliberately during the break — long focus sessions cut blink rate in half",
    "Hydrate eyes: keep artificial tears handy if you wear contacts or work in dry air",
    "Screen brightness matched to the room (lower it at night, raise it in daylight)",
    "Dark mode / reduced brightness for late sessions (not full-dark on a full-bright screen)",
    "If eyes burn or blur, that's the signal — take the break now, not after this one more log line",
  ]}
/>

<Callout variant="tip" title="20-20-20 is the floor">
  The 20-20-20 rule prevents the worst eye strain, but it's the minimum. If you're deep in a long session, a 5-minute walk every hour beats twenty 20-second breaks. Your eyes, your back, and honestly your debugging — stepping away is where the answer often appears.
</Callout>

## 2. Hourly Thoracic Mobility

<ChecklistBox
  title="Hourly Thoracic Mobility"
  items={[
    "Every hour: stand and do 5 thoracic extensions (gentle backbends over a chair or standing)",
    "Cat-cow on the chair: 5 rounds to mobilize the whole spine",
    "Shoulder blade squeezes: 10 reps to counter the forward-hunch of typing",
    "Neck half-circles: slow, 3 each direction (no full circles — they can compress nerves)",
    "Doorway chest stretch: 30 seconds in a doorway to open the pecs and shoulders",
    "Side bends: 3 per side to decompress the lower back from sitting",
    "If you've been sitting > 90 minutes, stand and walk for at least 2 minutes",
  ]}
/>

## 3. Wrist & Forearm Drills

<ChecklistBox
  title="Wrist & Forearm Drills"
  items={[
    "Wrist flexor stretch: arm out, palm up, gently pull fingers back, 30s each side",
    "Wrist extensor stretch: arm out, palm down, pull hand down, 30s each side",
    "Wrist circles: 10 each direction, slow and controlled",
    "Prayer stretch: palms together in front of chest, lower slowly, hold 30s",
    "Finger spreads: spread fingers wide, then make a fist, 10 reps",
    "Forearm self-massage: 30 seconds each forearm to release typing tension",
    "If wrists ache: switch input device (trackpad ↔ mouse) or take a 5-minute typing break",
  ]}
/>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Don't push through wrist pain">
  Wrist pain during a long session is your body telling you the setup or the volume is wrong. Stretching helps tight muscles; it doesn't fix a too-high desk or 12 hours of typing. If pain persists past the session, fix the setup (see the ergonomic workspace checklist) before the next one.
</Callout>

## 4. Posture Re-Stack

<ChecklistBox
  title="Posture Re-Stack"
  items={[
    "Sit all the way back in the chair — pelvis against the backrest, not perched on the edge",
    "Feet flat on the floor or footrest (uncrossed legs)",
    "Ears over shoulders over hips — no forward head posture from leaning into the screen",
    "Shoulders down and back, not creeping up toward the ears under stress",
    "Re-check every time you come back from a break (stress pulls you out of alignment)",
    "If standing at a desk: weight evenly distributed, soft knees, not locked",
    "Switch between sitting and standing every 30–60 minutes if you have a standing desk",
  ]}
/>

## 5. Night-Shift Lighting

<ChecklistBox
  title="Night-Shift Lighting"
  items={[
    "Bias lighting: a soft light behind the monitor to reduce screen-to-room contrast",
    "Room not fully dark — a dim lamp reduces eye strain vs. a lone bright screen",
    "Screen color temperature warmed (night shift / f.lux / redshift) after sunset",
    "Blue-light filter or glasses for late sessions (reduces sleep disruption)",
    "Brightness lowered at night — a daylight-bright screen in a dark room strains the eyes",
    "Glare eliminated — no monitor reflecting a dark window or a bare bulb",
    "After the session: 10–15 minutes of dim, warm light before bed to wind down",
  ]}
/>

## 6. Session Recovery

<ChecklistBox
  title="Session Recovery"
  items={[
    "After a long session: 5-minute walk to reset the spine and clear the head",
    "Full thoracic + wrist mobility routine (5 minutes) to undo the hunch",
    "Hydrate — long focus sessions dehydrate you and tank concentration",
    "If on-call overnight: protect sleep the next day (dark room, limited caffeine after noon)",
    "Note any new pain location — track it; recurring pain means fix the setup, not just stretch",
    "Don't immediately start another long session — give your eyes and back a real break",
  ]}
/>
