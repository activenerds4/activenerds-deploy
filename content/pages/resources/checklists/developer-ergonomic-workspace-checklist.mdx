# Developer Ergonomic Workspace Setup Checklist

**Your desk is either helping you focus or quietly wrecking your back. Set it up once, correctly.**

Most desk pain isn't from the work — it's from the setup. A monitor 4 inches too low or a chair 2 inches too high compounds over an 8-hour session into neck, shoulder, and lower-back pain. This checklist calibrates the four measurements that matter: monitor, desk, chair, and input devices.

## 1. Monitor Position

<ChecklistBox
  title="Monitor Position"
  items={[
    "Monitor at arm's length (about 20–30 inches / 50–75 cm from your eyes)",
    "Top of the screen (top bezel) at or slightly below eye level — you look slightly down, not up",
    "Monitor centered directly in front of you (no neck twist to see your primary screen)",
    "For dual monitors: primary centered, secondary at an angle, both within eye-level range",
    "Screen tilted back ~10–20 degrees to match your natural downward gaze",
    "Brightness matched to room lighting (not a flashlight in a dark room)",
    "Glare eliminated — no window or light source reflecting off the screen",
  ]}
/>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Eye line at the top bezel">
  The rule of thumb: the top edge of your screen should sit at eye level. This puts your natural resting gaze about 15–20 degrees below horizontal — right in the middle of the screen. If you look up to read the top of your editor, the monitor is too low; raise it. Stacks of books work, a monitor arm is better.
</Callout>

## 2. Desk Height

<ChecklistBox
  title="Desk Height"
  items={[
    "Desk height set so elbows rest at ~90 degrees with shoulders relaxed (not raised)",
    "Wrists straight (neutral) when typing — not bent up or down",
    "Forearms roughly parallel to the floor when hands are on the keyboard",
    "Keyboard and mouse at the same height as or just below elbow level",
    "No contact pressure on the wrists' underside (use a wrist rest or float your hands)",
    "Standing desk height set separately (elbow rule applies standing too)",
    "Desk depth allows monitor at arm's length without the keyboard pushing you too far back",
  ]}
/>

## 3. Chair & Lumbar

<ChecklistBox
  title="Chair & Lumbar"
  items={[
    "Chair height set so feet are flat on the floor (or on a footrest), knees at ~90 degrees",
    "Thighs parallel to the floor (not angled steeply down or up)",
    "Lumbar support meets the natural curve of your lower back (small of the back)",
    "Backrest reclined ~100–110 degrees (slightly back, not bolt upright)",
    "Seat depth leaves 2–4 fingers of space between the seat edge and the back of your knees",
    "Armrests support elbows at ~90 degrees without forcing shoulders up",
    "Feet supported — use a footrest if your feet don't reach the floor comfortably",
  ]}
/>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Calibrate the lumbar curve">
  Lumbar support only works if it lands in the right spot — the inward curve of your lower back, around belt level. Too high and it pushes your mid-back forward; too low and it does nothing. Sit all the way back in the chair, then adjust the support height until it fills the natural curve without forcing you forward.
</Callout>

## 4. Input Devices

<ChecklistBox
  title="Input Devices"
  items={[
    "Keyboard positioned so wrists stay neutral (split/ergonomic keyboard if you pronate)",
    "Mouse close to the keyboard — no reaching that flares the elbow",
    "Vertical/ergonomic mouse considered if you have wrist or forearm pain",
    "Wrists floating, not planted on the desk or wrist rest while typing",
    "Input devices at or just below elbow height (no shoulder shrug to reach them)",
    "Trackpad or mouse used without gripping tension in the hand",
    "Hotkeys / shortcuts used to reduce repetitive mouse movement",
  ]}
/>

## 5. Lighting & Environment

<ChecklistBox
  title="Lighting & Environment"
  items={[
    "Ambient lighting balanced with screen brightness (no harsh contrast)",
    "Task lighting from the side, not directly overhead or behind the monitor",
    "Window positioned to the side, not directly behind or in front of the monitor",
    "Room temperature comfortable for sustained focus (not too warm = drowsy, too cold = tense)",
    "Noise managed (headphones, white noise, or a quiet space for deep work blocks)",
    "Hydration within reach (a water bottle on the desk beats forgetting to drink)",
    "Air quality / ventilation adequate for long sessions (stuffy rooms tank focus)",
  ]}
/>

---

## 6. Daily Reset

<ChecklistBox
  title="Daily Reset"
  items={[
    "Re-check monitor height weekly (it drifts if you share a desk or move a laptop)",
    "Re-check desk height after any chair or standing-desk change",
    "Take a 20-20-20 visual break every 20 minutes (see the on-call reset checklist)",
    "Stand or walk for 2+ minutes every hour (even a perfect setup isn't meant for 8 hours still)",
    "End-of-day stretch: thoracic extension + wrist flexor drills (2 minutes)",
    "Weekly: photograph your setup from the side and check your posture against this list",
  ]}
/>
