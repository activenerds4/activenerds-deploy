Maya Chen is a senior machine learning engineer who has spent years building training pipelines, optimizing inference, and fighting with GPU memory. She writes the kind of tutorials she wished existed at 2am during a model training run — practical, skimmable, and honest about the parts the docs skip.

Somewhere between debugging OOM errors and waiting for epochs to finish, Maya discovered that five-minute desk stretches were the difference between finishing a sprint intact and burning out by Wednesday. She weaves movement tips into her technical content because, well, it would be hypocritical not to.

Her philosophy: tech content shouldn't read like a textbook, and wellness content shouldn't read like an Instagram influencer's caption. Meet people where they are — at their desk, deep in a problem, with maybe five minutes to spare.
