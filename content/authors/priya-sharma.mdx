Priya Sharma leads a DevSecOps team and has lived through enough on-call rotations to know that "just push through it" is not a sustainable strategy. After burning out twice, she got serious about habit science — not the productivity-influencer kind, but the evidence-based, small-changes-compound kind.

Her security content is practical and policy-as-code focused: supply chain hardening, threat modeling, compliance automation — the stuff that actually makes cloud workloads safer. Her habit-building content is for people whose schedules are unpredictable, high-stress, and full of context switches.

Priya's approach: you don't need a perfect routine. You need a resilient one that survives a 3am page and still has room for a walk the next morning.
