Marcus Webb is a Kubernetes platform engineer who built and operated clusters at scale — until a back injury from years of bad desk posture sent him down the ergonomics rabbit hole. Now he writes about both, with a tone that can only be described as "learned the hard way."

His cluster ops guides are battle-tested, not theoretical. His gear reviews come from actually using the standing desk, the under-desk treadmill, and the weird posture corrector for months before recommending them. If something is overhyped, he'll tell you. If something genuinely helped, he'll tell you that too.

Marcus believes platform engineering and ergonomics have more in common than people think: both are about building systems that don't quietly destroy you over time.
