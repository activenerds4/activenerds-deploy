You'll spend more time in your office chair than in your bed. Yet most programmers spend $2,000 on a monitor and $80 on a chair. This guide covers everything you need to know to buy a chair that supports 8+ hour coding sessions without wrecking your back.

## What Makes a Chair Ergonomic

### Adjustability Dimensions

A truly ergonomic chair adjusts in these dimensions:

1. **Seat height**: Feet flat on floor, thighs parallel to ground
2. **Seat depth**: 2-4 fingers between seat edge and back of knee
3. **Backrest height**: Lumbar support aligns with your lower back curve
4. **Lumbar support**: Adjustable height and depth
5. **Armrest height**: Elbows at 90 degrees, shoulders relaxed
6. **Armrest width**: Arms close to body without pinching
7. **Tilt tension**: Reclines smoothly with your body weight
8. **Tilt lock**: Locks in reclined position for reading

A $100 "ergonomic" chair from Amazon typically adjusts in 2-3 of these. A $1,000+ chair adjusts in all 8.

### Lumbar Support Types

**Fixed lumbar**: Built into the backrest at a set height. Works if you're average height and weight. Doesn't work for everyone.

**Adjustable height lumbar**: Can move up and down to match your spine. Better, but depth is still fixed.

**Adjustable height + depth**: Can move up/down and in/out. This is what you need for a custom fit.

**Dynamic lumbar**: Moves with you as you shift positions. Found in premium chairs (Herman Miller Embody, Steelcase Gesture). The best option for long sessions.

### Seat Pan Depth

The seat pan (the part you sit on) needs to be the right depth:

- **Too deep**: Presses against the back of your knees, restricting circulation
- **Too shallow**: Doesn't support your thighs, increasing pressure on your sit bones
- **Right depth**: 2-4 fingers of space between the edge and the back of your knee

Look for chairs with adjustable seat depth (seat slider). This is a feature often missing in budget chairs.

## Premium Tier ($1000+)

### Herman Miller Embody

The Embody is designed by ergonomists specifically for long-duration sitting. Its pixelated support surface adapts to your spine's micro-movements.

**Specs:**
- Pixelated support layer (139 pixels that adapt to your spine)
- Narrow backrest (allows arm movement)
- Adjustable seat depth
- Tilt limiter with flexible forward tilt
- Fully adjustable armrests (height, width, pivot)
- 12-year warranty
- Made in USA

**Price:** $1,495 (base) - $1,895 (fully loaded)

**What you get for the price:**
- The best spinal support of any chair on the market
- Dynamic support that moves with you
- 12-year warranty (they'll replace any part that fails)
- Excellent build quality and materials
- High resale value (used Embody sells for $600+)

<Callout type="info">

## Buying Tips

### Try Before You Buy

If possible, sit in the chair before buying:

- **Herman Miller showrooms**: Free to visit, all chairs available to try
- **Steelcase dealers**: Find a local dealer for test sitting
- **IKEA**: Markus is available in stores
- **Office furniture stores**: Often carry Branch and HON

If buying online, check the return policy:
- **Herman Miller**: 30-day return
- **Steelcase**: 30-day return (through dealers)
- **Branch**: 30-day return
- **IKEA**: 90-day return (best policy)

### Warranty Importance

A chair's warranty tells you about build quality:

| Brand | Warranty | What It Covers |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| Herman Miller | 12 years | All parts and labor |
| Steelcase | 12 years | All parts and labor |
| Branch | 7 years | Structural components |
| HON | Lifetime | Limited (frame, mechanism) |
| IKEA | 10 years | Frame and mechanism |
| Amazon Basics | 1 year | Manufacturing defects |

A 12-year warranty means the manufacturer expects the chair to last 12+ years. A 1-year warranty means they expect it to break.

### Used Premium Chairs

The used market is the best way to get a premium chair at a discount:

- **Craigslist**: Search "Herman Miller" and "Steelcase" — offices closing or upgrading often sell chairs at 50-70% off
- **Facebook Marketplace**: Same as Craigslist, often with photos
- **eBay**: Refurbished chairs with warranty
- **Office liquidators**: Search "office furniture liquidators [city]" — they buy from closing offices and sell cheap

A used Herman Miller Embody in good condition: $600-$800 (vs $1,495 new).

<Warning>
When buying used, check the casters (wheels), armrest pads, and cylinder (does it sink when you sit?). These are the most common wear items and are replaceable but add to the cost.
</Warning>

## Long-Session Comfort

For programmers doing 8+ hour sessions, these features matter most:

1. **Dynamic lumbar support** — moves with you, doesn't lock you in one position
2. **Breathable material** — mesh prevents sweat buildup during long sessions
3. **Adjustable seat depth** — prevents knee circulation issues
4. **Armrest adjustability** — reduces shoulder strain when typing
5. **Smooth tilt** — allows position changes without standing
